How it works

Three steps, about a minute.

  1. 1

    Pick your route

    Choose where you are leaving from and where you need to be. Every campus connects to every other campus.

  2. 2

    Choose a departure

    Buses leave on the hour, from 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM Central Time. Pick the hour that fits your timetable.

  3. 3

    Pay and board

    Up to 25 seats in one booking. Show the pass on your phone with your HCC ID.
